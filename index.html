Why Bakelorium

Allergen and substitution aware

Recipes flag known allergens and suggest swaps, so you know what to watch for.

No account needed

No account, no email, no signup, no login required. Open the app and start making.

Offline first

Designed to work without an internet connection, in the kitchen or on the go.

No AI to distract you

No AI features inside the app to pull your focus while you cook.

Local catalog of foods

A built-in catalog of foods and ingredients, no internet lookup required.

Metric and imperial

Weight-first measurements, in metric or imperial, your choice.
